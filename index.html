Part 1 — What this app is for

This app helps you:

  • Add money to play (we call that Recharge).
  • Take money out when you are allowed to (we call that Redeem or Cash out).
  • Move balance between game accounts (Transfer).
  • Change your game password (Reset password).
  • See Rewards, your games, and past requests (Activity).

You use taps on the screen. If you are stuck, use Support or Chat if your app has it.

Part 2 — Bottom menu (five main areas)

After you log in, you usually see five tabs at the bottom. Here is what each one is for:

Home Your main shortcuts: Recharge, Redeem, Transfer, Reset password, and sometimes call support or help.
Rewards Bonuses: referrals, daily rewards, promo codes, and special limited-time offers.
Play Your linked game accounts and adding more games when the app allows.
Activity A list of everything you asked for — waiting, done, or not accepted — with status for each.
Profile Your account details and settings.
Note: Chat may open from an icon and might not show as a normal bottom tab.

Part 3 — Recharge (add money)

Goal: Put money on one of your game accounts.

Steps

  1. Start. On Home, tap Recharge (or the word your app uses for “deposit”).
  2. Amount. Use the number pad. Enter how much you want. Tap Continue or Next.
  3. Game account. Tap the one username this payment is for. Continue.
  4. Payment method. Tap one option (for example Cash App or another app). Continue.
  5. Check the summary. Read amount, game, and method. Enter a promo code only if the box is there and you have a code. Tap Confirm when it looks correct.
  6. Wait if the app says so. You may see “waiting” or “pending assignment.” Stay on the screen. Do not pay yet until the app tells you to.
  7. Pay when told. The app shows payment details (tag, ID, or QR code) and often a timer. Pay inside that time and the amount the app shows (unless support says otherwise).
  8. Proof. If the app asks for a screenshot or photo of your payment, upload it.
  9. Check Activity. See if your recharge is pending, completed, or needs attention.
Remember: Going back and changing the amount may restart the flow. If the timer runs out, you may need a new request. Fix the wrong game before you pay — after payment, fixing is harder.

Part 4 — Redeem (cash out)

Goal: Ask to take money out of a game account when the rules allow.

  1. On Home, tap Redeem or Cash out.
  2. Tap the game username you want. The app shows limits if any. If you cannot continue, read the message on the screen.
  3. Enter how much (and tip if asked). Stay within the maximum shown.
  4. Choose where you receive the money (payment method list).
  5. Read the summary and confirm if it is correct.
  6. Follow the next screens. Use Activity to watch status.

Part 5 — Transfer

Goal: Move balance from one game account to another when allowed.

  1. On Home, tap Transfer.
  2. Choose from which account.
  3. Choose to which account.
  4. Enter amount and finish the confirm steps.
  5. Check Activity for pending or done.

Part 6 — Reset password

Goal: Get a new password for a linked game account.

  1. On Home, tap Reset password.
  2. Pick the game account that needs the reset.
  3. Follow the steps. Read any codes or messages slowly.
  4. Check Activity. Save your new password somewhere safe.

Part 7 — Rewards tab

Tap Rewards (often a gift icon) at the bottom.

Daily reward

If you can claim today, tap Claim. The message tells you where the money goes (often a reward balance first).

Referral (invite friends)

You may see a link or code to share. Rules for the bonus are explained in the app or by support.

Reward wallet

Some bonuses sit in a wallet first. The app says when you can move them to a game (see Part 8).

Special time offer

Read minimum deposit, percent bonus, and end time. If you claim, the app may open Recharge for your next deposit with that offer.

Promo code

Type the code, tap Apply. If it works, the app usually opens Recharge with that promo tied to your next steps.

Part 8 — Add rewards to game (simple rules)

Sometimes the app blocks moving rewards into the game. Messages are in normal language, for example:

  • You need more finished deposits first (the app may mention a number like five).
  • Your last deposit was too long ago — you need a new deposit before rewards can go to the game.
What to do: Read the on-screen message. Complete a normal Recharge if that is what it asks for. If it still fails, contact Support.

Part 9 — Play (games) tab

  1. Tap Play.
  2. Under My games, see accounts you already use.
  3. Under other sections, you may add a new game — follow the steps on the screen.

You need the correct game account linked before Recharge and Redeem make sense.

Part 10 — Activity tab

  1. Tap Activity.
  2. Tap a row to open details if the app allows.

Simple words for status:

  • Pending / waiting — not finished; wait or follow instructions.
  • Completed / done — finished successfully.
  • Rejected / failed — not approved; read the reason or ask support.

A red number on the tab can mean something needs your attention.

Part 11 — Profile

Open Profile for your name, email, security, and options like face or finger login if offered. If you change password or email, follow any verification messages.

Part 12 — Chat / support

Use support when you are stuck on waiting too long, a timer ended, money looks wrong or missing, or you do not understand a message.

Tell support: what you tapped, what you see now, and send a screenshot if you can.

Part 13 — Small word list

Part 14 — Quick FAQ

Stuck on “waiting”

Wait a reasonable time. Open Activity. If nothing changes for a long time, use Support.

Timer ran out before I paid

You may need a new recharge. If you already sent money, contact Support with proof.

Wrong game

Before paying: go back and fix it. After paying: Support with proof.

Bonus did not show

Check Rewards and Activity. Some bonuses need approval or a finished deposit first.

Forgot login

Use Forgot password on the sign-in screen if you see it, or ask Support.

Part 15 — Revival bonus & “after you're back”

Simple guide to how a comeback bonus fits next to normal rewards and deposits. Exact days and rules are set by your team in the system.

What is Revival bonus?

If someone stops playing for a long time — no deposits, cashouts, or certain other activity — your team sets how many days that is (often around 14). When they deposit again after that quiet period, they may get a special comeback bonus called Revival bonus. It is meant to welcome people back after they have been away.

What happens on the first deposit after they have been away long enough?

If they qualify, that deposit can get Revival bonus instead of the normal small bonus or signup rules.

What about signup bonus?

After that comeback situation, normal rules apply again for later deposits — signup only if your rules still say they qualify.

What about “add to game” and the “5 deposits” idea?

Some reward money may only unlock after the player does enough completed deposits.

When someone properly gets Revival on a comeback deposit, the idea is to treat them like a fresh start for that counting: only deposits after that revival moment should count toward the next five — not old deposits from months ago.

When is Revival “officially counted” for the system?

Two different moments are easy to mix up:

Comeback deposit created When the player creates the revival deposit in the app, the system can save the revival record early so the “comeback line” exists.
Deposit finished When that deposit is fully finished in the back office, only then does it count toward numbers like “how many deposits toward five.”
Remember: A record can appear early, but some numbers only move when the deposit is completed.

What if they cash out or transfer after coming back?

After Revival, if they take money out (redeem or transfer), that changes what offers they can get next time — same idea as normal: once they have cashed out, signup-style offers often stop until your rules say otherwise.

Before Revival, old cashouts do not keep blocking them forever in the “fresh start” story — the comeback deposit is the new starting point for how the app treats the next period.

Revival — quick FAQ (non-technical)

Why did someone not get Revival?

Common reasons: they were not actually quiet long enough, the team’s revival offer is turned off, they already used it if repeats are not allowed, the deposit was too small, or there is not enough past activity in the system to measure “quiet time” from.

Why did they get a normal bonus instead of signup after Revival?

Signup has extra checks (like balance rules). If signup does not pass, the app falls back to the normal payment bonus — that is expected, not a random bug.

Does Revival happen on every deposit?

No. It is for the comeback moment. Later deposits use normal rules unless they go quiet again long enough and your settings allow it again.
